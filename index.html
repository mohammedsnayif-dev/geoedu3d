STRIKE & DIP

Enter the bedding orientation using the right-hand rule (RHR). The dip direction is calculated automatically and shown with strike and dip arrows on the 3D model. This is an orientation illustration, not a measurement of the modeled formations.

Enter strike and dip, then click Calculate.

Model convention: North = −Z; East = +X. Strike follows the right-hand rule, with the bedding dipping 90° clockwise from strike. Horizontal bedding (0° dip) has no unique strike.

Burial history & thermal maturity

Educational linear burial and constant gradient model. Temperature thresholds: oil 60–120°C, gas 120–200°C, overmature above 200°C. Ro proxy uses peak temperature only; real maturity depends on time, kinetics and kerogen type. Linked overmature source disables new generation; it does not model preservation of earlier charge.
